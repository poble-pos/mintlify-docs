Choose the customer’s preferred options when adding an item to an order.

**Where:** POS device → sales screen → menu item

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the item">

Choose a menu item with pre-configured options. Its available option groups appear in a window.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the options">

Select the required choices, such as **Hot** or **Iced**. Review any additional prices and the selection limit displayed for each group.

<Frame caption="Item options window with Hot and Iced choices and Add To Cart">
  <img src="/images/sales-payments/item-options.png" alt="Item options window with Hot and Iced choices and Add To Cart" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step title="Add the item to the order">

Select **Add To Cart**, then check the item and selected options in the order panel.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The options shown depend on the groups assigned to that menu item.
</Note>
