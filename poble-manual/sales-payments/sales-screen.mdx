Browse categories, select menu items, and review the current order.

**Where:** POS device → sales screen

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose a menu category">

Select a category at the top of the menu, such as **Coffee**. The available items appear below.

<Frame caption="Sales screen with menu categories, items, order summary, and payment controls">
  <img src="/images/sales-payments/sales-screen.png" alt="Sales screen with menu categories, items, order summary, and payment controls" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step title="Add an item">

Select the item the customer wants. If an options window appears, choose the relevant options and select **Add To Cart**.

</Step>
<Step title="Review the order">

Check the items in the order panel on the right. Review quantities, prices, and the total before proceeding to payment.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Make any item changes, discounts, or notes before completing payment.
</Tip>
