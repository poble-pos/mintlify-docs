Adjust an item’s quantity or remove the selected item from the current order.

**Where:** POS device → sales screen → item quantity

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the quantity controls">

In the order panel, select the quantity beside the item name, such as **x1**.

</Step>
<Step title="Change the quantity">

In **Enter Quantity**, use **−**, **+**, or the keypad to enter the new quantity. Select **Change** to apply it.

<Frame caption="Enter Quantity window with quantity controls, Change, and the delete icon">
  <img src="/images/sales-payments/quantity-and-void.png" alt="Enter Quantity window with quantity controls, Change, and the delete icon" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step title="Void an item when needed">

To remove the selected item, use the red delete icon in the quantity window. Follow any further prompts, then review the order and updated total.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Check the selected item before removing it. To cancel the entire order, use **Cancel Order** instead.
</Warning>
