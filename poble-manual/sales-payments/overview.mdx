Use these guides for everyday sales on your POBLE POS, from opening the sales screen to reviewing an order and choosing a payment method.

## Follow the sales workflow

1. Select **Start Sales** on the POS main screen.
2. Add menu items and choose any item options.
3. Review quantities, item discounts, and notes.
4. Check the order total and proceed with **Cash**, **Card**, or **Checkout**.

<Tip>
Menu setup and taking orders are separate tasks. Use [Menu Management](/poble-manual/menu-management/overview) to create categories, items, and option groups before taking orders.
</Tip>
