Complete the payment screen to finish account setup. The Notion manual describes a **30-day free trial**; review the offer and terms shown in your checkout before starting.

## Enter Payment Details & Start Trial

<Steps>
<Step title="Review the checkout summary">

Check the selected plan, add-ons, **Total after trial**, and **Total due today**.

</Step>
<Step title="Enter contact and card details">

Complete the contact information and card fields, including card number, expiry date, CVC, and cardholder name.

</Step>
<Step title="Enter billing details">

Complete the billing address and the business or ABN information requested by the payment form.

</Step>
<Step title="Start your trial">

Check your details and the displayed charges, then select **Start trial**.

</Step>
</Steps>
