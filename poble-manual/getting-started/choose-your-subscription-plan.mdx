Review the plans available on **Choose Your Subscription Plan** and choose the option that covers your business needs.

## Choose Your Subscription Plan

<Steps>
<Step title="Compare the available plans">

Read each plan’s feature list and displayed monthly price. The source example shows a **Basic plan** with dual screen options, takeaway features, menu management, order management, and basic reports.

</Step>
<Step title="Select a plan">

Select the plan you want to use. The selected plan is marked **Selected**.

</Step>
<Step title="Review optional features">

Continue to **Customise with Add-ons** to choose any extra features you need.

</Step>
</Steps>
