After creating your company, set up the first store linked to your POBLE account.

## Set Up Your Store

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose how to enter store details">

On **Set up store**, use **Create a store with the same details as the company** when your store and company share the same details. If they differ, clear the checkbox and enter your store details.

</Step>
<Step title="Add your store logo">

Use the logo upload control to add an image for your store.

</Step>
<Step title="Review the store details">

Check that the details belong to the location you are setting up, then continue to the subscription stage of the setup flow.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Create your first store and add its details and logo.">
  ![Poble Logo Text Correction](/images/Poble-logo-text-correction-1.png "Poble Logo Text Correction")
</Frame>

<Note>
You can create additional locations later from **Stores** using **Add Store**.
</Note>
