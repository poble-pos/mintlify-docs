Add optional features to your selected plan during subscription setup.

## Customise with Add-ons

<Steps>
<Step title="Review the add-ons">

Open **Customise with Add-ons**. The source example includes kiosk, Uber Eats integration, extra POS devices, KDS, table booking, waitlist, table, loyalty, and online order options.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the features you need">

Select the add-ons relevant to your business. Review the description and displayed monthly charge for each selection.

</Step>
<Step title="Check the order summary">

Review **Order Summary** and **Total** to check your selected plan and add-ons.

</Step>
<Step title="Continue to payment details">

Use the trial or checkout control shown beneath the order summary to continue to the payment screen.

</Step>
</Steps>
