The Notion guide describes deactivation for a store that is closed or no longer in use. Review the on-screen warning carefully before proceeding.

## Deactivate Store

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the store">

Choose the store you intend to deactivate and open **Store Settings**.

</Step>
<Step title="Open Danger Zone">

Expand **Danger Zone** and find **Deactivate Store**.

</Step>
<Step title="Review the consequences">

The source screen says **Delete the store from the store lists** and **Close all of store’s account information**. Check that this action is appropriate for your location.

</Step>
<Step title="Deactivate the store">

Select **Deactivate** only when you are ready to proceed. Read and respond to any further prompts shown by POBLE.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Use the Danger Zone controls to deactivate a store.">
  <img src="/images/store-settings/deactivate-store.png" alt="Use the Danger Zone controls to deactivate a store." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
The source does not explain data retention, billing effects, or reactivation. Confirm those details with POBLE support before relying on deactivation for a temporary closure.
</Warning>
