Set the regular trading hours for the selected store in **Store Settings**.

## Business Hours

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the correct store">

Choose the store from the company/store selector, then select **Store Settings**.

</Step>
<Step title="Set daily opening and closing times">

Expand **Business Hours**. Enter the **Open** and **Close** times for each day you need to configure.

</Step>
<Step title="Add a break if needed">

Use the break control beside the day to add **Break start** and **Break end** times. Use the delete control if a listed break is no longer needed.

</Step>
<Step title="Review the weekly schedule">

Check the daily hours and break times, then apply your changes using the controls available on the page.

</Step>
</Steps>
