---

Add a message when your hours differ from the usual schedule, including public holidays, special trading hours, or temporary closures.

## Business Hours Message

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the message section">

Select the relevant store, open **Store Settings**, and find **Business Hours Message**.

</Step>
<Step title="Write the notice">

Enter a clear message with the affected date and trading hours. For a closure, include when you expect to reopen.

</Step>
<Step title="Save the message">

Select **Save** below the message field.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Tell customers about temporary changes to trading hours.">
  <img src="/images/store-settings/business-hours-message.png" alt="Tell customers about temporary changes to trading hours." />
</Frame>

<Tip>
Example: “We will be closed on 25 December and reopen on 26 December at 9 am.” Update the message when it is no longer relevant.
</Tip>
