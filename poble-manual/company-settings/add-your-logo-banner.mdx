Manage your company logo and banner from the **Business** tab in **Company Settings**.

## Add Your Logo & Banner

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the image settings">

Select **Company Settings**, open **Business**, and find **Images**.

</Step>
<Step title="Upload a logo">

Use the upload control under **Logo Image**. The screen specifies a **1:1** image ratio and explains that the logo appears in the top-left corner of the header.

</Step>
<Step title="Upload a banner">

Use the upload control under **Banner Image**. The screen specifies a **3:1** image ratio and explains that the banner is used on the sign-in page.

</Step>
<Step title="Review your branding">

Check both image previews and apply any changes using the controls available on the page.

</Step>
</Steps>

Use a square logo and a wide banner so each image fits the ratio shown in the settings.
