Use the employee list to view staff and begin adding another administrator.

## Add Admins & Manage Employees

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the employee list">

Select **Employees**, then **Employee List**.

</Step>
<Step title="Review your staff">

Use the search field or **Add Filter** to find a staff member. Review the **Name**, **Email**, **Backoffice Role**, and **Status** columns.

</Step>
<Step title="Begin adding an administrator">

Select **Add Employee**. Complete the staff details requested in the form and choose the administrator role when adding an admin.

</Step>
<Step title="Review access">

Check that the person’s role matches the access they need. Use the permission guides below when configuring staff access.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Add administrators and view staff from Employees.">
  <img src="/images/company-settings/add-admins-manage-employees.png" alt="Add administrators and view staff from Employees." />
</Frame>

<Note>
Administrator and employee access should match each person’s responsibilities.
</Note>
