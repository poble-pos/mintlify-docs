Review your business information in **Company Details**.

## Update Company Details

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Company Details">

Select **Company Settings**, open **Business**, and expand **Company Details**.

</Step>
<Step title="Review the business information">

Check the company **Name**, **Code**, and **Primary Email** shown in the section. Update the fields that are editable and need changing.

</Step>
<Step title="Apply the updates">

Review the changes and use the page’s available controls to apply them.

</Step>
</Steps>

</Frame>

</Tip>
