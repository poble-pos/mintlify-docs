title: "Set Role Permissions"
description: "Configure the actions each staff role can perform."
---

Use **Roles** to choose which actions staff can perform in each permission category.

## Set Role Permissions

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Roles">

Select **Roles** from the company sidebar.

</Step>
<Step title="Expand a role">

Open the role you want to configure. The source screen shows **Franchise Manager**, **Shop Manager**, **Shop Owner**, and **Staff**.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose permitted actions">

Review each permission category and select the required **View**, **Edit**, and **Delete** actions where available. The source example includes employee, menu management, royalty management, and sales report categories.

</Step>
<Step title="Save the role">

Select **Save** after reviewing your changes.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Configure the actions each staff role can perform.">
  <img src="/images/company-settings/set-role-permissions.png" alt="Configure the actions each staff role can perform." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
Changing a role’s permissions also changes the permissions of existing employees with that role, as stated in the Roles screen. Review the impact before saving.
</Warning>
