Use **Stores** in the company view to see locations linked to your business.

## Manage Stores

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Stores">

Select **Stores** from the company sidebar.

</Step>
<Step title="Find a store">

Use the **Active** tab to view active stores. Use the search field to locate a store by name. The source screen also shows **Brand**, **All**, and **Inactive** tabs.

</Step>
<Step title="Add a location">

Select **Add Store** and enter the details requested for the additional location.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="View your company’s stores and add another location.">
  <img src="/images/company-settings/manage-stores.png" alt="View your company’s stores and add another location." />
</Frame>

<Tip>
Check the selected company before adding a store so the location is linked to the correct business.
</Tip>
