Use **Profile** to keep administrator information up to date.

## Manage Admin Profiles

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Profile">

In the company view, select **Profile** from the sidebar.

</Step>
<Step title="Update the details">

On the **Details** tab, review and update **Name**, **Email**, **Contact (optional)**, and **Address** as needed.

</Step>
<Step title="Save the profile">

Select **Save** to apply your changes.

</Step>
</Steps>
