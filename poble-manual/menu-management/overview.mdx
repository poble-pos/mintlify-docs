---
title: "Menu Management"
description: "Create menus, manage options, and configure the POS menu for your stores."
---

Create and maintain your POBLE menus in the backoffice or directly on a POS device. Choose the guide that matches where you are working.

## Choose your workspace

| Workspace | Use these guides for |
| --- | --- |
| Backoffice — Company | Company menu categories, items, available stores, and option groups. |
| Backoffice — Store & Franchise | Categories and items associated with a selected location. |
| POS Device | Menu categories, items, and options managed on the POS itself. |
| Menu Configuration | Option mapping, printer mapping, prepaid items, menu layout, and discounts. |

<Tip>
Start with categories, then create menu items and option groups. Assign the options to the relevant items before arranging the POS menu layout.
</Tip>
