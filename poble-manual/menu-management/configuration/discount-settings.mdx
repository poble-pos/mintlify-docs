Create discount options for your POS from the company backoffice.

**Where:** Backoffice → company view → POS → POS Settings → Discount Options

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Discount Options">

Select your main company, then open **POS → POS Settings**. Find **Discount Options**.

</Step>
<Step title="Enter the discount details">

Open the form for adding a discount option. Enter **Name** and **Description**, then choose **Rate** or **Amount** under **Type**.

<Frame caption="Company POS Settings and Add Discount Option form">
  <img src="/images/menu-management/discount-settings.png" alt="Company POS Settings and Add Discount Option form" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step title="Set the value and add">

Enter the discount rate or amount for the selected type. Review the details and select **Add**.

</Step>
<Step title="Review available discounts">

Check the entry in **Discount Options** and review existing options when changes are needed.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use a descriptive name so staff can identify the correct discount at checkout.
</Tip>
