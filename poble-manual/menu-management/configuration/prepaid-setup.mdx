Create gift card or prepaid items from the POS menu tools.

**Where:** POS device → Menu Management → Prepaid Setup

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Prepaid Setup">

Select **Prepaid Setup** in the menu management sidebar, then select **+** to begin adding an item.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the item type">

On **Basic Info**, select **Gift Card** or **Prepaid**, as appropriate.

<Frame caption="Prepaid Setup form with Gift Card and Prepaid choices">
  <img src="/images/menu-management/prepaid-setup.png" alt="Prepaid Setup form with Gift Card and Prepaid choices" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step title="Enter the item details">

Choose **Category** and enter **Item Name**, **Price**, and **Tax Option**. Add an image, barcode, **POS Display Name**, and **Description** if needed.

</Step>
<Step title="Review and create">

Review **Print Options** and **Advanced Options** if required, then select **Create**.

</Step>
</Steps>
