Create company menu items and update their names, prices, categories, and other details.

**Where:** Backoffice → company view → POS → Menu Settings

<Steps>
<Step title="Select a category">

Choose a category from the list. Its menu items appear beside it. Select **Create Menu Item** to add an item.

<Frame caption="Coffee category with menu items and Create Menu Item button">
  <img src="/images/menu-management/company-item-list.png" alt="Coffee category with menu items and Create Menu Item button" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step title="Enter the item details">

Choose **Item Type** and enter **Name** and **Price**. Add an image, **POS Display Name**, **Description**, **Tags**, and barcode details as needed. Review **Tax Options**, then select **Create**.

<Frame caption="Create Menu Item form in the company backoffice">
  <img src="/images/menu-management/company-create-item.png" alt="Create Menu Item form in the company backoffice" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step title="Edit an existing item">

Open the item from its category. Update the relevant fields. Use **Change Category** when you need to move it to another category, then apply your changes using the page controls.

<Frame caption="Company menu item details and Change Category button">
  <img src="/images/menu-management/company-edit-item.png" alt="Company menu item details and Change Category button" />
</Frame>

</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Field labels can differ between the create and edit screens. Review the selected item and its price before applying changes.
</Note>
