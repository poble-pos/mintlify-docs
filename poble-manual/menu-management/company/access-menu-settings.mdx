Choose your company and open the menu settings in the backoffice.

**Where:** Backoffice → company view → POS → Menu Settings

<Steps>
<Step title="Check the company and store selector">

Use the selector at the top of the backoffice to check which company or store you are managing. For company-level menu setup, work in the company view.

<Frame caption="Company selector and available stores">
  <img src="/images/menu-management/company-selector.png" alt="Company selector and available stores" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step title="Open Menu Settings">

Expand **POS** in the sidebar and select **Menu Settings** to view your menu categories and items.

<Frame caption="Menu Settings in the company backoffice">
  <img src="/images/menu-management/company-menu-settings.png" alt="Menu Settings in the company backoffice" />
</Frame>

</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Check the selected company or store before making changes. Use the store guides when managing a specific location.
</Tip>
